How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
