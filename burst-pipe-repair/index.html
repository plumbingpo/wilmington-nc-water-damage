Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Day or night, Wilmington Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across Wilmington and the nearby communities. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Have a licensed plumber inspect your plumbing system every few years for wear and corrosion
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat
  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry
  • Leave the heat on when you travel in winter so indoor pipes stay above freezing
