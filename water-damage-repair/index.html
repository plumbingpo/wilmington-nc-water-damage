How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Run the bath fan during showers and for a while afterward so rebuilt walls stay dry.
  • Take pictures of damaged materials prior to removal, since the insurer may want them later.
  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.
  • File the written scope and photos of the completed repair with your household papers.
  • Save spare tile and flooring, plus paint color details, for matching later repairs.
  • Curing time for paint, floor adhesive and caulk should pass before the room sees water or hard use.

Warning Signs for Water Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Door casings and baseboards have warped or pulled off the wall surface.
  • Hardwood boards have cupped, crowned or lifted and did not flatten after drying.
  • Water under the tile left cracked grout lines or tiles that rock when stepped on.
  • You see brown stains, peeling or bubbles in paint along the path the water took.
  • Since the water damage, certain doors drag on the jamb or miss the latch.
  • Seams are opening, or edges are curling and swelling, on vinyl plank or laminate.
  • The lower part of cabinet sides or bases feels soft, looks swollen or is peeling.
